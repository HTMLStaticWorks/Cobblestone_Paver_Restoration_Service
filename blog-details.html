How to Identify Failed Sealers

James Stone
Restoration Expert | 5 min read

Water absorption is the first sign of a failing sealer. If you notice that your pavers turn dark when wet instead of water beading on top, it's time for a re-seal.

Outdoor stone surfaces are constantly under attack from UV rays, acid rain, and freeze-thaw cycles. A good sealer typically lasts 3-5 years depending on the quality and the environment.

The Water Test

Pour a glass of water on various parts of your driveway. If the water is absorbed within 60 seconds, the protective barrier is gone. If it beads, you're still protected.
