Deep Pressure Washing & Steam Cleaning

Our specialized pressure washing service is designed to remove years of grime, moss, and atmospheric pollution without damaging the delicate stone surface.

We use industrial-grade steam cleaning equipment that operates at high temperatures but controlled pressures. This ensures that the biological growth (like algae and lichen) is killed at the root, preventing fast regrowth.

Our Methodology

1. Surface Assessment

We identify the stone type and choose the correct pressure/temp settings.

2. Pre-treatment

Eco-friendly solutions are applied to loosen stubborn organic stains.
