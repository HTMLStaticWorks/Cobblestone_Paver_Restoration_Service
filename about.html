Our Proven Methodology

We follow a strict, structured approach to ensure every stone restoration project meets our highest standards of excellence.

1

Detailed Assessment

We conduct a thorough inspection of your stone surfaces to identify damage, drainage issues, and deep stains, creating a customized restoration plan.

2

Intensive Deep Cleaning

Using industrial-grade rotary surface cleaners, we extract years of embedded dirt, oil, and moss without damaging the surface integrity.

3

Premium Sealing

Once fully dry, we apply a commercial-grade penetrating sealant that locks in color, stabilizes joint sand, and protects against future damage.
